## October 2026

Explore recent improvements to practice and troubleshooting:

- **Inspect computer resources before choosing a fix.** [What is a Computer?](/prerequisites/zero-to-terminal/module-0.1-what-is-a-computer/) clarifies how to record your system's specs, distinguish RAM from file storage, and check a diagnosis before changing anything.

- **Handle Git interruptions and recovery.** Revisit [Worktrees and Stashing](/prerequisites/git-deep-dive/module-5-worktrees-stashing/) to compare ways to set work aside and practice returning to it.

- **Follow declarative changes.** [Declarative vs Imperative](/prerequisites/philosophy-design/module-1.2-declarative-vs-imperative/) explains recorded intent, ownership and reconciliation, with guided practice.

- **Explore Kubernetes configuration and boundaries.** [ConfigMaps and Secrets](/prerequisites/kubernetes-basics/module-1.6-configmaps-secrets/) compares configuration changes with what a running container uses. [Namespaces and Labels](/prerequisites/kubernetes-basics/module-1.7-namespaces-labels/) clarifies system namespaces, shared label conventions and how quotas work with defaults.

- **Plan foundational study and practice terminal files.** [Plan Study Scope by Goal](/prerequisites/philosophy-design/module-1.3-what-we-dont-cover/) links to related cloud, networking and tooling lessons while helping you choose study depth. [Files and Directories](/prerequisites/zero-to-terminal/module-0.4-files-and-directories/) uses a disposable workspace and helps you diagnose permissions and filenames. Plan your next step with [Prerequisites](/prerequisites/) and the [Platform entry checklist](/platform/#do-not-start-here-yet).

For detailed past updates, see the [full changelog history on GitHub](https://github.com/kube-dojo/kube-dojo.github.io/blob/main/docs/changelog-history.md).
