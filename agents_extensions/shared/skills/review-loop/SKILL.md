# Review loop

- The accountable lead freezes the scope, acceptance criteria, non-goals, review brief, base and head before review; record the planned rounds, automatic ceiling and spent invocations, and carry them forward with the findings.
- A review stream is either one PR across all its heads and branches, or one whole-page source/editorial audit across all its seeds and packets. These two kinds keep separate counters. Relabeling a stream or changing its reviewer never starts a new counter.
- Allow up to five independent review invocations automatically per stream; R-numbers count invocations. Continue necessary fixes and exact-head or exact-page/seed re-review through R5 without requesting permission merely because the initial plan used fewer rounds. Honor an explicit smaller user cap or stop; later explicit user authorization may raise the cap or resume current work, but never repairs earlier verdicts. Failed, incomplete and inconclusive invocations count; preparation that stops before invocation does not. Changing the head, seed or packet name never resets the counter.
- Resolve an available reviewer from live routing under AGENTS.md; embedded dated model tables are not live routing authority. Use [cross-family-reviewer](../cross-family-reviewer/SKILL.md) for a real independent review outside the author's model family at the exact pushed head or page/seed. Record reviewer identity, family, revision, verdict and evidence on the PR or owning issue within the invocation budget when authorized.
- Keep stable finding IDs, evidence, severity and dispositions. Resolve material findings; a supported disagreement remains open and blocking pending resolution under drive-epic's escalation rule, and is not approval. Stop after a valid approval at the unchanged head or page/seed with no unresolved material findings; defer optional nits rather than starting another round solely for them.
- A changed head or page/seed invalidates its prior exact-revision verdict: review the new revision and relevant fixes within the remaining budget, including when optional edits caused the change. Preserve the original brief and finding ledger.
- A valid approval for this unchanged pushed head or page/seed returned within budget remains usable on the final allowed invocation. Exhaustion alone grants no clearance: missing, failed, contested or inconclusive review, an unavailable reviewer, or no valid verdict before the budget ends requires reporting the evidence, remaining findings and responsible owner; do not manufacture approval.
- Exceed five only when an explicit user authorization covers the additional invocation; an existing larger grant needs no repeated request. Otherwise obtain authorization for the concrete remaining review. Automatic continuation grants no permission to skip independent identity, complete evidence, material-finding resolution, CI, publication or acceptance gates, and never permits constructing or rebinding a source receipt.
- For curriculum, distinguish a scoped delivery review from full-page acceptance, which requires the applicable source, rubric and practice evidence. Report what was and was not reviewed.
